  • react-native-select-contact相关
    1. Cannot open the contact selector twice
    添加queries相关代码 如下:
    AndroidManifest.xml
    1
2    <manifest >
3        <application></application>
4        <queries>
5            <intent>
6            <action android:name="android.intent.action.VIEW" />
7            <category android:name="android.intent.category.DEFAULT" />
8            <data android:mimeType="vnd.android.cursor.dir/contact" />
9            </intent>
10        </queries>
11    </manifest>

  • unable to initiate PIF transfer session (operation in progress?)
    移除文件 ~/Library/Caches/org.swift.swiftpm
    1    rm -rf ~/Library/Caches/org.swift.swiftpm
2
  • A problem occurred starting process 'command 'npx'
    如果本地有正确配置node，那大概率是Android Studio 的Gradle JDK问题。 检查 Setting->Building,Executing,Deployment->Build Tools->Gradle->Gradle JDK是否正确，按照RN官网配置，我们应该选择Zulu17.0.12相关
